---
title: FAQ
description: Common questions about reviews, limits, models, and billing
---

<Accordions type="multiple">
  <Accordion title="Does CodeTurtle approve or merge my PRs?">
    No, never. CodeTurtle posts a review - that's all. It has no merge, approve, or push capability, even for admins. You (or branch protection) decide whether to merge.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did my review fail?">
    The usual causes, in order of likelihood: (1) the model provider had an outage and all fallbacks failed too, (2) your BYOK key was revoked or expired, (3) the PR was too large to fit any model's context and compression failed, or (4) plan limits. The failure is always explained in a comment on the PR and in the [Reviews](/reviews) page - check the error text there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is my code used to train models?">
    No. We don't train models and we don't fine-tune on your code. Generation goes to the model provider you selected, under their API terms, one review at a time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What counts as a review?">
    Each posted review on a pull request - including re-reviews after a push. Comment replies to `@codeturtle` don't count against your quota.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How does the 3-commit limit work on Free?">
    Free reviews cover PRs with up to 3 commits. If your PR has 4+ commits, the review still runs but notes that coverage is partial; upgrade to Pro for full review regardless of commit count.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use CodeTurtle on private repos?">
    Yes - personal and organization repos both work. Connect them from [Repositories](/repositories); organization repos need your GitHub account to have admin access on the repo.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to my free reviews if I upgrade mid-month?">
    Pro applies immediately, including for the current cycle. Your free-tier counter resets naturally on your billing date - it doesn't matter once you're on Pro.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I remove CodeTurtle from a repo?">
    Open [Repositories](/repositories) and disconnect it. CodeTurtle deletes the webhook and purges the indexed embeddings for that repo. Review history is kept unless you ask us to delete your account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can my team share one CodeTurtle account?">
    Reviews attach to GitHub identities. Each collaborator who wants reviews needs their own account - but Pro is $4/mo, so usually everyone just joins. Enterprise handles org-wide rollout and dedicated support.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is the default model routed through OpenRouter?">
    Model availability changes constantly. Routing through OpenRouter's auto-router means your reviews keep working even when a specific provider model retires mid-month - the router simply lands on the next best live model under your plan.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
