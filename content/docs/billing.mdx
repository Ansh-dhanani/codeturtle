## How billing works

Checkout and subscription management run through **[Polar](https://polar.sh)**. Polar is the merchant of record; CodeTurtle never sees your full card number.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Click **Upgrade** on [Billing](/billing) and complete Polar checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Polar fires a signed `subscription.created` webhook to CodeTurtle within seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    CodeTurtle validates the Standard Webhooks signature, matches the checkout to your account, and bumps your plan to Pro/Enterprise - usually before the checkout page returns.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    You get the Pro badge in the dashboard, unlimited usage, and BYOK unlocked immediately. No re-login required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Downgrades go through the opposite path: `subscription.canceled` snaps limits back to Free at the end of the billing period. Cancellation is one click in **Manage** on the Billing page.

## Usage & limits

- [Billing](/billing) shows this month's review count, plan, and reset date
- Free usage resets on your billing cycle; Pro has no visible counters because there is no limit
- If a review can't run due to plan limits, CodeTurtle tells you why on the PR comment and links to upgrade

## Invoices & receipts

Polar issues every invoice and receipt automatically - check the email you used at checkout, or the **Manage** portal on [Billing](/billing) for past invoices.
