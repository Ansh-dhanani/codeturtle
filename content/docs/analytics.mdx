The [Analytics](/analytics) page turns your review history into decisions: is the codebase getting healthier, where do the bugs concentrate, and which repos need attention?

## KPIs at a glance

The top row answers the four questions leadership actually asks:

- **Total reviews** - volume over the selected window
- **Average score** - 0-10 quality trend across all completed reviews
- **Issues found** - total problems CodeTurtle surfaced (critical / warning / info)
- **Completion rate** - completed vs failed review runs
- **Positives flagged** - reinforcement signal for good changes

## Charts

| Chart | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **12-week score trend** | Rolling average of review scores - are PRs getting safer over time? |
| **Severity breakdown** | Critical / warning / info share of all findings |
| **Weekly issue volume** | Stacked bar of findings per week, by severity |
| **Per-repo table** | Reviews, average score, issues, suggestions, completion rate per repository |

Score trend and issue charts use a trailing **12-week window**; the contribution graph on the dashboard shows a rolling **12 months** (switchable to any year since your GitHub account was created).

## Insight cards

Three auto-generated callouts summarize what changed recently:

- **Score trend** - up/down movement and what drove it
- **Busiest repository** - where review volume concentrates
- **Top warnings** - the most frequent finding class this period

## Reports

Click **Report** on the Analytics page to download a Markdown report for the current scope (everything or a single repository). Reports include KPIs, trend data, severity breakdowns, and per-repo summaries - built for standups, postmortems, and audits.

## The Reviews list

The [Reviews](/reviews) page is the raw feed behind the analytics:

- Every review with status (**Completed / Failed / In progress**), score, and summary
- Filter by repository and status
- Expand any row to read full findings
- Upvote / downvote individual findings - signal that tunes future reviews
- Kick off a fix PR from review findings

<Callout title="Data freshness">
  Analytics update as reviews complete - usually within seconds of a review posting to GitHub. Failed review runs are counted separately and never skew your average score.
</Callout>
